# Cynefin (Snowden)

- **Layer / Taxon:** thinking · problem-classification (reasoning move)

> **Reassignment note:** Cynefin lives in the master guide's Strategy section (LAYER 1) but is deliberately reassigned here to the **thinking** taxon. It classifies the *problem* (Clear / Complicated / Complex / Chaotic / Confusion) BEFORE any framework is selected — a reasoning move, not an org operating layer. This is the one deliberate placement that diverges from the source document.

- **Origin** `[FACT]`: Dave Snowden, IBM, 1999; mainstreamed via 2007 HBR (with Mary Boone). [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cynefin_framework]
- **Structure (5 domains):** Clear (sense-categorize-respond) · Complicated (sense-analyze-respond) · Complex (probe-sense-respond) · Chaotic (act-sense-respond) · Confusion/Disorder.
- **SWOT:** **S** stops applying linear solutions to complex problems · **W** abstract, hard to operationalize fast · **O** essential for GenAI (classify as complex/chaotic) · **T** defaulting to "Clear"-domain solutions out of comfort.
- **Case study:** European Commission (JRC) used it to shift from analysis-paralysis to act-sense-respond in systemic crises. [source: https://thecynefin.co/library/managing-complexity-and-chaos-in-times-of-crisis/]
- **Evidence** `[INDIRECT]` — qualitative validation.
