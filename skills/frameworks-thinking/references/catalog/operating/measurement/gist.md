# GIST Model

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · measurement
- **Layer:** Gemini files under Measurement/Execution.
- **Origin** `[SIGNAL]`: Itamar Gilad (ex-Google). [unverified — 2017 origin date]
- **Structure:** `Goals (OKRs) → Ideas → Steps (experiments — discovery) → Tasks (engineering — delivery).`
- **SWOT:** **S** dismantles feature-factories, enforces evidence before heavy eng · **W** needs a mature culture willing to kill bad ideas mid-cycle · **O** GIST Scorecard self-audits HiPPO-reliance · **T** execs demanding committed delivery dates for unvalidated "Ideas."
- **Case study — cautionary:** **Google (Gmail/Google+ integration)** — pushed on executive consensus not evidence; **users rejected it, years of eng scrapped.** `[COI — AUTHOR EQUITY]` [self-reported: https://www.financemagnates.com/executives/insights/product-management-perspectives-lessons-from-gmails-failed-integration-with-google/]
