# AARRR / Pirate Metrics

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · measurement
- **Origin** `[FACT]`: Dave McClure, 500 Startups, 2007. [source: https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/startup-metrics-for-pirates-long-version/89026]
- **Structure:** `Acquisition → Activation ("aha") → Retention → Referral → Revenue.`
- **SWOT:** **S** shifts from vanity to actionable growth levers · **W** linear, misses compounding growth loops · **O** find the single biggest SaaS bottleneck · **T** over-investing in Acquisition (leaky bucket) when Activation/Retention is the real gap.
- **Case study:** **Dropbox** — referral (500MB/referral) viral loop → **CAC cut ~60%, ~3900% growth in 15 months.** [self-reported: https://growsurf.com/blog/dropbox-referral-program/] [source: https://growsurf.com/blog/dropbox-referral-program/]
- **Evidence:** Gemini `[DIRECT]` / ChatGPT `[INDIRECT]` — a funnel heuristic, not a general management framework.
