# Balanced Scorecard + Strategy Maps

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · measurement
- **Origin** `[FACT]`: Robert Kaplan & David Norton, 1992. [source: https://hbr.org/1992/01/the-balanced-scorecard-measures-that-drive-performance-2]
- **Structure:** Four perspectives — `Financial | Customer | Internal Processes | Learning & Growth` — linked by a **Strategy Map** (cause-and-effect).
- **SWOT:** **S** stops execs mortgaging the future to hit quarterly financials · **W** bloats into 50+ KPIs → dashboard fatigue · **O** Strategy Maps visually link learning→process→customer→financial · **T** building the scorecard but not linking it to resource allocation.
- **Case study:** Mobil adopted BSC and linked compensation to balanced metrics across decentralized units, turning around competitive position. [case cut 2026-07: specific figures unverifiable]
- **Evidence** `[DIRECT]` — historically ubiquitous. "One of the most serious frameworks for connecting strategy to measurement."
