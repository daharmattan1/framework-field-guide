# KPIs (Leading vs. Lagging) + North Star + Health Scores

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · measurement
- **Origin** `[FACT]`: core analytics (KPIs) / Sean Ellis (North Star) / Customer Success discipline (health scores).
- **Structure:** `North Star (one number for core value) ← Leading KPIs (predictive inputs) → Lagging KPIs (financial results) + Health Scores (account-level R/Y/G churn risk).`
- **SWOT:** **S** universal alignment; a good North Star prevents short-term-revenue-over-value · **W** single-metric obsession blinds to secondaries (optimize "time spent," ignore profit) · **O** automate health scores via telemetry vs. CSM gut · **T** mistaking vanity metrics (total registered accounts) for leading KPIs (DAU).
- **Case study:** **Amplitude** aligns product strategy to "Weekly Querying Users." **Cautionary:** metric-gaming risks (local metric optimization at the expense of customer reality). [case cut 2026-07: specific examples unverifiable]
- **Evidence:** Gemini `[DIRECT]` (canon); ChatGPT flags **North Star `[INDIRECT]`** — academic evidence (Jeunen et al., Chou et al., 2024–26) shows NSMs are often delayed/statistically insensitive. "Good orienting star, poor sole instrument."
