# The Bowtie Model

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · measurement
- **Origin** `[SIGNAL]`: Winning by Design.
- **Structure:** `Acquisition (Awareness→Education→Selection→Close) → the Knot (commitment/handoff) → Expansion (Onboarding→Impact→Growth→Advocacy).` Closed-Won is the midpoint, not the end.
- **SWOT:** **S** cures the Sales→CS momentum drop-off · **W** needs sophisticated RevOps data integration · **O** captures that majority of SaaS LTV is post-sale · **T** siloed departments refusing shared accountability for the right side.
- **Case study:** **Canva** adopted the Bowtie (via SPICED) to align GTM/Sales/CS. `[COI — VENDOR]` [source: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/claydank_five-years-ago-i-walked-into-the-canva-office-activity-7249197287543877632-FXSI]
- **Evidence** `[INDIRECT]` — strong SaaS logic, heavily promoted by its creator consultancy `[COI — FRAMEWORK OWNER]`.
