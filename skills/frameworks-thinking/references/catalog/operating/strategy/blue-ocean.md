# Blue Ocean Strategy

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · strategy
- **Origin** `[FACT]`: W. Chan Kim & Renée Mauborgne, INSEAD, 2005. [self-reported: https://www.blueoceanstrategy.com/authors/]
- **Structure:** Strategy Canvas + Four Actions (Eliminate/Reduce/Raise/Create).
- **SWOT:** **S** breaks the value-vs-cost trade-off · **W** very high risk, true market size unknown pre-launch · **O** ideal for saturated "red ocean" industries · **T** imitators turn blue oceans red fast.
- **Case study:** **Nintendo Wii (2006)** — cut hi-def/complex controls, created motion controls + approachability, outsold Sony + Microsoft combined. ⚠️ **Both sources flag this as the survivorship-bias exemplar** — ChatGPT treats Blue Ocean as a strategy-layer *cautionary case for reputation exceeding causal evidence*; Gemini presents the Wii more cleanly. *Adjudication: present the Wii, but with the survivorship caveat foregrounded.* [source: https://www.theregister.com/2008/06/13/us_console_sales_may_08/]
