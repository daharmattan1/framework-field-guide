# Playing to Win (Lafley/Martin)

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · strategy
- **Origin** `[FACT]`: A.G. Lafley & Roger Martin, P&G / HBR Press — Gemini dates **2013**; ChatGPT gives no year. [source: https://www.amazon.com/Playing-Win-Strategy-Really-Works/dp/142218739X]
- **Structure (cascade, each choice constrains the next):** `Winning Aspiration → Where to Play → How to Win → Capabilities → Management Systems.`
- **SWOT:** **S** forces rigorous trade-offs and specificity · **W** skewed toward large CPG firms · **O** reverse-engineer the cascade to test logic · **T** the five questions answered independently, not as an interlocking system.
- **Case study:** **P&G Olay** — shifted Where-to-Play to a "masstige" ($18.99) segment, grew **$800M → $2.5B**. **Cautionary — GM Saturn:** chose to "participate" not "win" vs. Toyota/Honda; **burned ~$20B, discontinued.** `[COI — AUTHOR EQUITY]` [self-reported: https://www.fool.com/investing/general/2013/02/27/playing-to-win-how-procter-gamble-increased-its-ma.aspx]
