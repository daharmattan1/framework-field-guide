# Three Horizons (McKinsey)

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · strategy
- **Origin** `[FACT]`: McKinsey, *The Alchemy of Growth*, 1999. [source: https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/enduring-ideas-the-three-horizons-of-growth]
- **Structure:** `H1 core (metric: profit) / H2 emerging (revenue growth) / H3 future options (validated learning)` — manage concurrently, not sequentially.
- **SWOT:** **S** protects long-term R&D from short-term profit demands · **W** misread as a rigid 5–10-yr timeline · **O** shift allocation ratios by industry phase · **T** applying H1 ROI metrics to H3 experiments kills innovation.
- **Case study:** **Reliance Jio** moved an H3 tech (4G) to H1 dominance in 4 years. **Cautionary — Kodak:** held digital-camera patents (H3) but strategically deprioritized them to protect film (H1) → bankruptcy. [source: https://strategy-engine.pages.dev/chapters/chapter-20-growth-frameworks/]
- **Evidence:** ⚠️ conflict — Gemini `[DIRECT]`, ChatGPT `[INDIRECT]` (best as a discussion tool, not a capital-allocation engine). [source: https://lindeninnovation.com/three-horizons-of-innovation/]
