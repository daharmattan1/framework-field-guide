# MBO (Management by Objectives)

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · strategy
- **Layer:** ⚠️ **CONFLICT** — ChatGPT files under Strategy; Gemini under Alignment/Measurement. *Adjudication: Strategy-origin, but it is genuinely cross-layer; the ancestor of OKRs.*
- **Origin** `[FACT]`: Peter Drucker, *The Practice of Management*, 1954. [self-reported: https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/48018.The_Practice_of_Management]
- **Structure:** `Corporate objectives → unit → individual → periodic review → reward`.
- **SWOT:** **S** introduced managing by outcome not activity · **W** tight coupling to comp incentivized low-balling; ignored holistic org health · **O** its DNA lives on in OKRs · **T** reviewed only at annual appraisal.
- **Use / fails:** Stable, predictable, sales-driven environments with strong managerial follow-through. Fails in dynamic knowledge work or when individual targets sabotage collaboration.
- **The canonical critique:** **W. Edwards Deming** — when numerical targets overpower systems understanding, people optimize the target, not the system. This is the intellectual root of tensions #5 and #8.
- **Evidence** `[DIRECT]` for productivity association (stronger when CEO commitment is high); weak for universal generalization. Lifecycle: ChatGPT "Mature," Gemini "Declining."
