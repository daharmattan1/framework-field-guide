# Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · strategy (secondary: measurement)
- **Layer:** ⚠️ conflict — ChatGPT Strategy-only; Gemini Strategy + Measurement.
- **Origin** `[FACT]`: Clayton Christensen, HBS, 2000s.
- **Structure:** `Situation → Motivation/the Job → Expected Outcome (functional + emotional + social).`
- **SWOT:** **S** shifts from demographics to behavioral drivers, reveals non-obvious competitors · **W** needs deep qualitative interviewing · **O** major product/marketing clarity · **T** optimizing features vs. the holistic job.
- **Case study:** **McDonald's milkshake** — flavor tweaks yielded zero growth; JTBD revealed commuters "hired" thick shakes to beat a boring drive. [source: https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/clay-christensens-milkshake-marketing]
- **Evidence:** ⚠️ conflict — Gemini `[DIRECT]` ("widely validated"); ChatGPT `[INDIRECT]` (spread owes as much to memorable framing as to hard evidence). *Adjudication: INDIRECT.* [self-reported: https://strategyn.com/jobs-to-be-done/]
