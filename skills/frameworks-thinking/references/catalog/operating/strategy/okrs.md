# OKRs (Objectives + Key Results)

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · strategy
- **Origin** `[FACT]`: Andy Grove adapted MBO at Intel (1970s); John Doerr carried it to Google (1999); *Measure What Matters* (2018). [source: https://okrexperts.com/okr-method/okr-john-doerr/]
- **Definition:** A qualitative, ambitious Objective paired with 3–5 measurable Key Results (each a metric moving X→Y by a date), on a quarterly cadence, decoupled from compensation to enable moonshots (70% attainment = success). [source: https://okrfaq.com/introduction-to-okrs/what-are-moonshot-and-roofshot-okrs/]
- **Structure:** `Objective → KR1/KR2/KR3 (metrics) → Initiatives`.
- **SWOT:** **S** organizational focus + cross-functional alignment, shifts measurement from output to outcome · **W** degrades into a glorified to-do list if KRs measure activity not impact · **O** pairs with continuous performance-management tooling · **T** "set-and-forget" until quarter-end.
- **Use / fails:** Use for cross-functional alignment, aggressive growth, quick pivots. Fails when layered on BAU without freeing capacity, or with too many simultaneous objectives (violates focus).
- **Case study:** **Google** (adopted <1 yr old; stretch goals drove Chrome/Android) — but both sources flag survivorship bias (can't isolate OKRs from monopoly + talent). **Sears Holding (2013):** deployed to 20,000 employees, *initially failed* (no focus/check-ins), pivoted to localized tracking → **8.5% revenue lift.** [self-reported: https://www.shortform.com/blog/google-objectives-okrs/]
- **Combine:** `OP-4dx` (execution engine beneath), `OP-rapid`/`OP-daci` (decision clarity), `OP-prioritization` (RICE/WSJF portfolio), `OP-dora`/`OP-kpis-north-star` (measurement). **Conflict:** `OP-mbo`-style individual bonuses; rigid annual planning; full-stack systems that already specify cadence (`OP-eos-traction`, `OP-hoshin-kanri`).
- **Evidence:** ⚠️ **CONFLICT** — Gemini `[DIRECT]` (case-backed); ChatGPT `[INDIRECT]` (strong adoption, weak independent causal evidence). *Adjudication: treat as INDIRECT — the folklore outruns the science.* [source: https://aaltodoc.aalto.fi/bitstreams/e5e85c11-2ee0-42e0-b9be-f7996b4fb276/download]
