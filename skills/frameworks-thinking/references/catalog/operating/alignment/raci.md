# RACI

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · alignment
- **Origin** `[SIGNAL/FACT]`: responsibility-assignment matrices, 1950s matrix management; no single originator; embedded in PMI practice.
- **Structure:** Task × Role → **R**esponsible / **A**ccountable / **C**onsulted / **I**nformed.
- **SWOT:** **S** eliminates ambiguity, prevents decision-by-committee · **W** bureaucratic bloat; matrices go stale on pivot · **O** automate in Jira/Asana · **T** multiple Accountables (diffuses ownership) or over-Consulting (paralysis).
- **Use / fails:** Cross-functional projects stalled by unclear ownership. Fails for routine BAU, or when people forget assignments within a week; clarifies *participation* without clarifying who *decides*.
- **Case study:** Clear RACI cuts feedback loops and accelerates delivery. Conversely, ambiguous ownership in cross-functional work (no assigned Accountable) causes delays and rework. [case cut 2026-07: unverifiable]
