# RAPID (Bain)

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · alignment
- **Origin** `[FACT]`: Bain & Company, 2006. [source: https://hbr.org/2006/01/who-has-the-d-how-clear-decision-roles-enhance-organizational-performance]
- **Structure:** **R**ecommend / **A**gree (veto) / **P**erform / **I**nput / **D**ecide. Unlike RACI, explicitly about *decisions*; addresses "consensus masquerading as decision-making."
- **Use / fails:** Exec-level decision rights / who owns the call. Fails when overused → bureaucratizes everyday decisions. [self-reported: https://www.bain.com/insights/rapid-decision-making/]
- **Combine:** `OP-okrs` (the decision-clarity layer beneath goals), `OP-raci`, `OP-daci` · **Conflict:** —
