# Hoshin Kanri (Catchball)

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · alignment
- **Layer:** ⚠️ conflict — ChatGPT Strategy; Gemini Alignment.
- **Origin** `[FACT]`: postwar Japanese quality mgmt / Yoji Akao; TPS lineage.
- **Structure:** `Vision → breakthrough objectives → annual objectives → local plans → PDCA`, visualized by the **X-Matrix**; **Catchball** = leadership throws an objective down, management catches/analyzes/throws back, iterating to consensus.
- **SWOT:** **S** operationally realistic plans + deep frontline buy-in · **W** slow cycle (6–8 weeks), no native weekly rhythm · **O** embed OKRs at the bottom for weekly accountability · **T** fake catchball (feedback ignored, targets mandated anyway) destroys trust.
- **Case study:** **GE Appliances** used the X-Matrix → **12% cost reduction + 20% faster time-to-market in 24 months.** [self-reported: https://www.isixsigma.com/hoshin-kanri/case-studies-successful-implementation-of-hoshin-kanri-in-global-organizations/]
