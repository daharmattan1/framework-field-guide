# Cascading OKRs

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · alignment
- **Origin** `[SIGNAL]`: corporate adaptation of Intel/Google OKRs.
- **Structure:** `Company O → Company KR = Dept O → Dept KR = Team O` (parent KRs become child Objectives).
- **SWOT:** **S** theoretical unbroken CEO→frontline line-of-sight · **W** massively bureaucratic (5-layer cascade takes weeks), ignores lateral alignment → competing silos · **O** replace strict cascade with "localized interpretation" · **T** command-and-control cultures using cascades to micromanage.
- **Case study:** **Sears Holding (2013)** — rigid cascade to 20,000 failed; localized focus → 8.5% revenue lift (same case as OKRs). [self-reported: https://okrs.com/2015/03/sears-holding-company-study-concludes-okrs-impact-the-bottom-line/]
- **Combine:** `OP-okrs` · **Conflict:** —
- **Evidence:** strict cascading fails, directional alignment succeeds [source: https://blog.tability.io/cascading-okrs-is-costly-and-you-should-avoid-it/]. Multiple independent practitioner sources (Tability, Caroli.org, Christina Wodtke/Medium, Weekdone, OKRs.com) converge on this position: rigid/direct cascading is widely reported as failing at scale (waterfall-like, doesn't scale) while directional/conversational alignment is recommended instead.
