# Theory of Constraints (Goldratt)

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · execution
- **Origin** `[FACT]`: Eliyahu Goldratt, *The Goal*, 1984. [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Goal_(novel)]
- **Structure — Five Focusing Steps:** `Identify → Exploit → Subordinate everything else → Elevate → repeat (prevent inertia).`
- **SWOT:** **S** forces systems thinking, prevents local optimization · **W** counter-intuitive for accounting that values 100% utilization · **O** ports to software (QA/CI-CD bottleneck) · **T** optimizing non-constraints creates excess WIP.
- **Case study:** **Bethlehem Steel (1994)** — losing money at 12+ week lead times; subordinated processes to the constraint → **lead times to 6 weeks, due-date performance >90%, positive return on net assets.** [self-reported: https://www.tocinstitute.org/theory-of-constraints.html]
- **Combine:** Kanban, DORA. **Conflict:** cost accounting + MBO (demand 100% utilization). Evidence `[DIRECT]` for operations.
