# Prioritization: RICE / ICE / MoSCoW / WSJF

- **Layer / Taxon:** operating · execution
- **Origins** `[FACT]`: RICE = Intercom (2018); ICE = Sean Ellis; MoSCoW = Dai Clegg (1994); WSJF = Don Reinertsen (cost-of-delay), adopted by SAFe. [source: https://www.intercom.com/blog/rice-simple-prioritization-for-product-managers/]
- **Structures:** RICE = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort · ICE = Impact × Confidence × Ease · MoSCoW = Must/Should/Could/Won't · WSJF = Cost of Delay / Job Size.
- **SWOT:** **S** forces a Confidence calc penalizing gut ideas; MoSCoW's "Won't" enforces boundaries · **W** "subjective guesses masquerading as hard math," biased to short-term wins · **O** trimming bloated backlogs, stopping HiPPOs · **T** "prioritization theater" (numbers manipulated to justify a pre-decided feature).
- **Case study:** Intercom RICE case (influencer-marketing feature, score of 2) unverifiable — cut specific. [case cut 2026-07: unverifiable]
- **Evidence** `[INDIRECT]` — Intercom itself warns RICE "should not be treated as an inflexible rule."
